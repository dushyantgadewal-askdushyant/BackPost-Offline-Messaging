# Using BackPost

## Conversations

**Chat History** lists previous conversations and flags unread activity. Incoming messages from another conversation leave your current screen in place. Open a thread to read it, search its messages, or long-press a message to copy it. You can also mark a conversation unread from its available actions.

A single check means sent. Two checks mean delivered. The two checks change color when the message is read on the peer phone. A queued message can wait for a connection. Open the conversation and check its connection status if delivery is taking longer than expected.

## Files

Tap the attachment icon, choose a file, and watch its progress in the conversation. Files up to 50 MB can transfer automatically between verified devices. For larger files, the sender waits for the receiver to accept before sending the contents. The maximum file size is 1 GB. Keep the phones nearby while the transfer runs. If you retry an interrupted transfer, it starts again from the beginning.

On Android 10 and newer, check **Downloads/BackPost** in the phone’s Files app. If BackPost could only save a private copy, use the conversation’s shared-files view to find it. Transfer status indicates whether the file finished saving.

## Devices and connections

Use **My Device** to change the name other people see and view your fingerprint. Open the information icon in a conversation to view the peer’s name, fingerprint, verification status, and app version when available. Manually disconnecting a paired device disables its automatic reconnection. Pair it again when you want to resume communication.

The conversation header shows the direct connection and identifies Wi-Fi or Bluetooth when Android reports it. “Wi-Fi” in this context refers to the nearby link between phones. The phones do not need a shared router or mobile data for the exchange.

## History and notifications

- Choose **7 days**, **90 days**, or **Forever** for message retention. These settings affect history on this phone.
- Delete a chat to remove that conversation from this phone. The other person’s copy is separate.
- Manage notification permission and message previews in Settings. Message previews are off by default.
- Mute an individual chat for **1 hour**, **8 hours**, or until you unmute it. Its messages can still appear in Chat History.
- Turn on **Available nearby** when you want a short background availability window on a supported phone. It lasts up to 15 minutes and shows an ongoing notification with a **Stop** action. Messages and notifications depend on an active nearby link.
